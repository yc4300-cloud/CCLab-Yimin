Hungry Sacabambaspis

Scientific Name:

Hungry Sacabambaspis

Discovered by / at:

Yimin Chen, 2022

Family:

Sacabambaspididae (primarily composed of other Sacabambaspis who look equally surprised).

Habitat:

Deep under the sea.Its habitat was primarily in shallow, coastal waters along the margins of the southern supercontinent Gondwana

Appearance:

Two round eyes, a triangle shaped mouth as if it’s gasping all the time.Blush face. A disproportionately large, flat, and armored head,with a round body and tail.

Key Characteristics:

  • They like to eat and they swim very slowly. They appear wherever there’s food and they try their best to wiggle their plump bodies to swim to the food.

They aggregate in groups and demonstrate intense food-oriented behavior. Their emergence correlates strongly with snack presence. Individuals occasionally float the ventral side upward (it depends on their mood.)
Put food in the water and they will appear. Watch them slowly wiggle toward it with great determination. They really want the food — they’re just not very good at swimming fast.
